What we do

We email you free AI packs: a map, runnable tools, and a walkthrough. Pack one is the Value Readiness Memo — asset vs. job, four capital scores, where value is trapped in the owner, three changes for this quarter. That is using AI on the actual company, not a lecture about the future.

More packs, meetups, and an advisor-in-a-pocket only if this one gets used. We would rather give away a useful kit than pretend we are an events company.

What we will not do

Pitch-fests. Fake accreditation. Selling tickets as the identity of the nonprofit. Taking equity. Manufacturing discipline in people who did not ask for it. Calling a readiness memo a valuation.
