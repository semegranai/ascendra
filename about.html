What we do

We email you free AI packs: a map of what to do, tools you can run, walkthroughs so you are not guessing. We start with a comprehensive read of the four sources of capital in the business — human, customer, structural, social. That first pack is the Value Readiness Memo. Later packs go across the rest of the operation.

If you advise owners, there is a page you can send to clients. We do not ask anyone to fire a working relationship for a machine.

What we will not do

Pitch-fests. Fake accreditation. Selling tickets as the identity of the nonprofit. Taking equity. Calling a readiness memo a valuation. Telling you to fire your CPA, your bookkeeper, or anyone else who already knows the business.
