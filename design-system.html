Design language · v1.0 · August 2026

Two rooms. Same house.

The public site — where owners pick up a toolkit — runs dark. It signals seriousness, mono precision, no pitch-fest. Once someone is on the list, or in a room, they enter the light mode — toolkit delivery, event-day materials, the memo they take home. Same type, same grid, same components. Warmer, brighter, hosted. The design system captures both. Visual language ported from Infra Capital Ventures; voice is Ascendra’s.

01 · Modes

The same design tokens with inverted foreground/background and warmer accents. An owner’s state should shift as they progress — from visitor to someone we’ve actually helped.

05 · Voice

Do

Direct. Specific. Adult.

  • "Author your legacy. Execute on your terms."
  • "A buyer is not paying for your calendar."
  • "Get the business off your cell phone."
  • "This is not a pitch-fest."
Don't

Hype. Event scarcity. Fake product.

  • Not: "Join the mastermind" · "limited seats" · "$97 investment"
  • Not: "small business" (say owner, local, operator)
  • Not: "unlock your potential" or advisor-day-rate cosplay
  • Not: tax-deductible claims, accreditation, or sale-price promises

06 · Principles

01

The tool is the product.

Show the skill working. Do not describe a dashboard. Consistency over cleverness.

02

Value before the room.

The list and the toolkit add value before any meetup. Events earn a date; they are not the identity of the site.

03

Data as texture.

Scores, captions, and monospace chrome earn seriousness. Never decorative — always specific (cell phone, bookkeeper, four capitals).

04

Warm on engagement.

Once they have the toolkit — or are in a room — surfaces shift to light mode. The system rewards showing up with warmth and hosting.

Use it

The tokens ship as two CSS files. Drop them in, everything follows.

site.css defines the dark defaults. site-light.css overrides via .mode-light on the body. All pages share the same components — only the tokens change.
