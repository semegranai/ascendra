Design language · v1.0 · August 2026

Light house. Gold torch.

Colors, type, and the seal come from the 2025 masterclass deck — not from another firm’s site. The public pages run on warm paper. Navy and gold show up as the ceremonial room: the footer, the ticker, event-day covers, the circular mark. Same components in both. This document is the single source of truth.

01 · The seal

The circular mark is the brand. Hand, torch, ring type. Do not redraw it. Do not drop it into a square crop.

Ring: Author Your Legacy · Execute On Your Terms

Center wordmark is cream on navy. Torch is gold. Use the PNG with a transparent field. Minimum 32px. On navy, keep the gold ring visible.

02 · Modes

06 · Voice

Do

Direct. Specific. Welcoming.

  • “Author your legacy. Execute on your terms.”
  • “A buyer is not paying for your calendar.”
  • “Get the business off your cell phone.”
  • “This is not a pitch-fest.”
Don’t

Vault energy. Event scarcity. Fake product.

  • Not: dark “institutional” chrome borrowed from another firm
  • Not: “join the mastermind” · limited seats · ticket math
  • Not: “small business” (say owner, local, operator)
  • Not: tax-deductible claims or accreditation

07 · Principles

01

Invite first.

The page is a table, not a gate. Light paper, readable type, a gold torch — not a dark funnel.

02

The tool is the product.

Show the skill working. Do not describe a dashboard. The transcript is the demo.

03

Value before the room.

The list and the toolkit add value before any meetup. Navy is for the seal and the footer, not the whole house.

Use it

Tokens live in one CSS file. The seal is a PNG. Everything else follows.

site.css is the source of truth. Light is the default. Navy is a surface, not a theme invert.
